Director’s Message

Prof Bhartendu Nath Mishra
Director of MPDREC, Gonda

| Director Message

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Rajkiya Engineering College, Gonda. As the Director, I am honored to lead this institution dedicated to academic excellence, innovation, and the holistic development of students. At REC Gonda, we aim to empower students with knowledge, skills, and values that prepare them to thrive in a rapidly changing global landscape. We believe in creating not just engineers but responsible citizens and visionary leaders. Our highly qualified and dedicated faculty foster an environment that promotes curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking. With modern infrastructure, advanced labs, and a supportive ecosystem, REC Gonda provides the perfect platform for students to achieve their dreams.

"Your attitude and efforts determine your altitude in life."

To the parents, thank you for trusting us with your child’s future. Let us work together to make REC Gonda a center of excellence and inspiration for generations to come. Wishing you a bright and successful journey ahead.
