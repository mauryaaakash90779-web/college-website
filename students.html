Scholarship

Following types of scholarships may be awarded to students.
  • Merit scholarship of Rs. 3000 is awarded to 10 percent of the total number of students of each B.Tech. branch by Uttar Pradesh Government.
  • At state level, students (general/OBC/SC/ST/Minorities) may apply the U.P. scholarship and Institute (tuition) fee reimbursement with a condition of low annual income of Parents’/Guardians’ from all sources, as per govt. rules.
  • Scholarships through ‘National Scholarship Portal’.
  • Financial aid is given to economically weaker students from Student Aid Fund.
  • Other Miscellaneous Scholarships offered by Private Trusts/Agencies as announced from time to time.
